Privacy Policy

This policy describes Hermes Personal, a private automation setup operated by the owner of maxim11odessa. It is intended for the owner's personal Google account, not public registration.

Data accessed and purpose

Depending on the Google permissions granted and the requested task, the setup may access email messages and metadata, calendar events, Drive files and metadata, documents and spreadsheets. It uses this information to perform the owner's requests and configured automations, including agenda summaries, email assistance, document work and file synchronization.

Local storage and synchronization

OAuth credentials and operational settings are stored on the owner's computer. Conversation history, task outputs and selected notes may also be stored locally. Files and notes may be synchronized with Google Drive and the owner's other devices. No Google credentials or private account data are intended to be included in this public website or its repository.

External processing and disclosure

Relevant portions of task data, including Google account content when needed for a task, may be transmitted to the external AI provider configured in Hermes Agent. Task inputs and outputs may also pass through configured messaging services such as Telegram. Those services process received information under their own policies and settings. The owner should review provider choices before requesting processing of sensitive content.

Google account data is not sold or used by this setup for advertising. This setup does not intentionally use Google user data to train a generalized AI model. The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. This statement does not replace the need to verify that the configured external providers and actual operations comply with those requirements.

Retention, deletion and access control

Retention depends on the owner's local history, note, backup and synchronization settings. Revoking Google authorization stops future authorized API access but does not automatically delete previously stored copies, backups or messages. The owner can remove local data, synchronized files and backups separately, and can revoke the application's access in Google Account settings. Any third-party copies are subject to the relevant service's deletion controls and retention policy.

Contact and changes

For questions, refer to the operator's GitHub profile. The operator should update this policy when the setup's data handling changes.
